I've been going through Patanjali's Yoga Sutras lately. This is the 2nd sutra of 1st chapter:

योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः — *yogaḥ cittavṛtti nirodhaḥ.*

Yoga is the stilling of the whirlpools of the mind. The vrittis — the same thought, the same wound, looping again.

---

Yoga asks us to become the drishta, the silent observer.

I'm just practising: notice a reaction, trace it to its root. Watched long enough, the whirlpool slows.
