Write a tweet for me about: $ARGUMENTS

## Instructions

Saloni has Twitter Premium (25,000 character limit per tweet). Prefer single long-form tweets over threads unless she specifically asks for a thread.

### Step 1: Understand the input

The user will share one of:
- A topic or experience they want to tweet about
- A rough draft or brain dump
- A reading note or book takeaway
- An opinion or observation

### Step 2: Ask clarifying questions if needed

If the topic is vague, ask:
- What happened? (the story)
- How did it feel? (the emotion)
- What's the takeaway? (the lesson)

### Step 3: Write the tweet

**Voice guidelines — Saloni's Twitter voice:**
- Personal and grounded, not preachy
- Opens with a hook (something that happened, a bold statement, or a feeling)
- Short punchy paragraphs with line breaks between them
- Mixes vulnerability with strength
- Connects personal experience to a universal truth
- Ends with a call to action, a question, or a one-line closer that lingers
- NO hashtags unless she asks for them
- NO emojis in the body — only at the end if it fits naturally

**Structure for long-form tweets:**
1. **Hook** — first 1-2 lines that stop the scroll
2. **Story** — what happened, make it vivid and personal
3. **Insight** — the lesson, the realization, the shift
4. **Universal truth** — why this matters beyond just Saloni's life
5. **Closer** — a line that stays with the reader

**For threads (only if requested):**
- Each tweet should work standalone AND flow as a sequence
- First tweet must hook — end with 🧵
- Last tweet should close with a thought or CTA

### Step 4: Offer variations

After writing, offer:
- A shorter version (if the tweet is long)
- A different angle (if applicable)
- Ask if she wants to adjust tone, add/remove details, or make it more personal
